Common questions

Straight answers before you hire us.

We work on contingency - no upfront attorney fees. If we don't recover compensation for you, you don't owe our legal fees. We'll explain percentages and costs clearly during your free consultation.
Texas follows modified comparative fault rules. You may still recover damages if you were less than 51% responsible. Don't assume you're barred - let us review the facts.
Most personal injury claims must be filed within two years of the injury, but exceptions exist. Evidence fades quickly - contacting us soon protects your rights.
Get medical care, call police if needed, photograph the scene, gather witness info, avoid admitting fault, and don't give a recorded statement to insurers before speaking with a lawyer.
